Our Story

A passion for dragons. A commitment to genetics. A legacy built one generation at a time.

Dragons Of The North began with a simple love for bearded dragons and grew into a passion for genetics, bloodlines, and responsible breeding.

Our goal is to produce healthy, quality dragons with carefully planned genetics while continuing to learn and improve with every generation.

Every dragon has a story, every pairing has a purpose, and every generation becomes part of the legacy we are building.
