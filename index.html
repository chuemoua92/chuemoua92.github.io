Our Story

Built from a passion for bearded dragons and a commitment to producing healthy, quality animals.

Dragons Of The North started with a simple love for bearded dragons and the desire to build something meaningful around them.

Our focus is on responsible breeding, strong genetics, proper care, and producing dragons that can thrive as both pets and future breeders.

Every pairing is planned with the goal of improving quality, maintaining healthy bloodlines, and producing unique genetics for fellow reptile keepers.
