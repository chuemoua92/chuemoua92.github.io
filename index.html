Our Story

From one dragon to a growing passion.

Dragons Of The North started with a simple love for bearded dragons and grew into a passion for responsible breeding, genetics and producing healthy, quality animals.

Our goal is more than producing beautiful dragons. We focus on proper care, thoughtful pairings and giving every dragon the best possible start before finding the right home.
